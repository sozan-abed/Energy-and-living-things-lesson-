Animals need plants for energy

Animals including humans cannot make their food. They must eat, or consume, plants or other animals to get their food and energy. Animals including humans are called .

Some animals eat plants only. We call animals that eat only plants . Zebras and snails are examples of herbivores.

🐯 Tiger Carnivore & Predator

Some animals eat other animals that eat plants. We call these animals . Carnivores eat herbivores. They also sometimes eat other carnivores. Tigers and owls are examples of carnivores.

Sometimes carnivores are called . The animals that carnivores kill and eat are called . For example, an owl that kills and eats a mouse is a predator. The mouse is the prey.

Animals that eat both plants and animals are called . For example, monkeys mostly eat plant leaves, fruits and seeds, but they also eat insects and other small animals.

Zebras graze on savanna grass — they are herbivores!

1. Why are plants called producers?

✅ Because plants make (produce) their own food using energy from sunlight!

2. Why are animals called consumers?

✅ Because animals cannot make their own food; they must eat (consume) plants or other animals to get energy.

3a. What is the difference between predators and prey? Give an example.

3b. In what way are predators and prey similar?

3c. Can a herbivore be a predator? Say why or why not.

✅ 3a: A predator hunts and eats other animals (e.g., an owl). Prey is the animal hunted and eaten (e.g., a mouse).

✅ 3b: Both predators and prey are animals and both are consumers!

✅ 3c: No, because herbivores only eat plants—they do not hunt or eat other animals.

Food chains

A shows the order in which animals eat plants and other animals to get energy. Food chains always contain a and at least one . Energy is always transferred from the producer to the consumer because the consumer eats the producer. Look at the examples.

In food chain 1, the corn is the producer and the hen is the consumer.
Identify the producer and the consumer in food chain 2.

We can show a food chain as a drawing. When we draw a food chain, we show the order in which food, and the energy it contains, are transferred from one living thing to the next. The order of living things in a food chain is always:

producer ➔ consumer

The arrows in the food chain show the direction in which the energy in the food is transferred.
This is the drawing of food chain 1:

corn ➔ hen

If a boy eats the hen in food chain 1, he is another consumer. So now the food chain is:

corn ➔ hen ➔ boy

1. Identify the producer and the consumer in food chain 2 (Grass & Goat).

✅ The grass is the producer, and the goat is the consumer (grass ➔ goat).

2. What do the arrows in a food chain show?

✅ The arrows show the direction in which food and energy move from one living thing to the next.
